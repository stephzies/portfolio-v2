02 Approach

Behind the experience

Startzie began with a problem I knew firsthand: managing everything I needed to do while keeping sight of what mattered. As I used the app, I noticed where the interface interrupted my flow: recurring tasks crowding a Category, task details pulling me away from what I was doing, and information competing for attention. Those observations shaped my design decisions and reinforced a principle I carry into my work: understand how the pieces fit together, make what matters easy to find, and let the experience feel natural.
